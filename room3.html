Top-Floor Luxury

The Azure Penthouse

₱13,900 / night • Up to 4 Guests

Perched at the highest point of Casa Maison Bleue, The Azure Penthouse is the ultimate expression of open-air Mediterranean luxury. Fusing indoor comfort with an expansive rooftop deck, this penthouse sanctuary features floor-to-ceiling glass walls that open up to an infinity sky pool, a cozy sunken fire pit lounge with signature royal blue cushions, an outdoor stone fireplace, and an outdoor copper soaking tub. Complete with a private glass-enclosed wellness room overlooking the ocean, it offers an unforgettable 360-degree coastal experience.

Signature Features & Layout

  • Sunken Fire Pit Lounge: An architectural centerpiece featuring built-in plush seating adorned with signature Mediterranean blue velvet cushions around a central fire bowl.
  • Glass-Rimmed Infinity Sky Pool: Suspended rooftop pool positioned directly against the horizon for uninterrupted sea and sky views.
  • Outdoor Stone Fireplace: A hearth built into natural stone masonry on the open terrace for evening relaxation.
  • Rustic Copper Soaking Tub: An open-air freestanding copper bathtub set against stone walls for romantic outdoor bathing.
  • Glass-Enclosed Wellness Suite: Private, light-filled spa room with glass walls overlooking the coastline for on-demand in-suite massage and body treatments.
  • Retractable Glass Doors: Full-height glass panels that slide away completely to blur the line between the indoor salon and the outdoor sky terrace.
