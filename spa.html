The Wellness Suite

Our Luxury Spa & Wellness Sanctuary

₱3,900 / session

Perched high above Bacuit Bay in El Nido, the Wellness Suite is designed as an intimate sanctuary for deep rest and rejuvenation. Combining Old-World Mediterranean craftsmanship with modern therapeutic design, it features crisp white stucco walls, natural stone masonry, and cobalt blue geometric tilework, inviting guests into a soothing space dedicated to organic sea-salt therapies and hydro-relaxation.

Signature Features & Layout
  • Mosaic Headboard Alcove: An arched accent wall featuring hand-laid mosaic tile patterns inspired by traditional Mediterranean zellige craftsmanship.
  • Blue Glass Moroccan Lanterns: Suspended ornate blue glass lanterns that cast soft, serene ambient lighting across the treatment area.
  • Private In-Suite Spa Shower: A full glass-enclosed rain shower set against warm travertine stone tile, equipped with brass fixtures and an overhead rainfall head.
  • Stone Niche Masonry: Recessed arched display niches lined with exposed natural stone, serving as structural art within the space.
  • Curated Terracotta Amphorae: Earthy, hand-crafted clay vessels and antique Mediterranean urns set within illuminated stone alcoves.
  • Organic Botanical Toiletries: Complimentary signature bath and body amenities infused with organic Palawan sea minerals, cold-pressed coconut oil, and calming island botanicals.
