Rooms, Suites & Penthouse

Wellness

Our Luxury Spa & Wellness Sanctuary

Step into a world of deep rejuvenation at our sanctuary, inspired by traditional Mediterranean bathhouses and tropical wellness rituals. Fine Dining

Restaurant & Fine Dining

Perched on the dramatic limestone cliffs of Palawan overlooking the turquoise waters of Bacuit Bay, Casa Maison Bleue Fine Dining offers a culinary journey where European elegance meets tropical refinement. Enjoy an unforgettable evening on our open-air terrace enveloped in warm lantern light, crisp white linens, and sweeping sunset vistas.

Our menu pairs the finest locally-sourced Palawan seafood and tropical ingredients with classic French-Mediterranean techniques and a sommelier-curated collection of rare vintages. Whether dining under the stars or watching the sun sink into the ocean, every detail is crafted for an intimate, world-class dining experience.
