About Casa Maison Bleue

Perched atop the dramatic limestone cliffs of El Nido, Palawan, Casa Maison Bleue is a sanctuary where French Riviera refinement seamlessly blends with tropical Philippine paradise. Overlooking the crystal-clear turquoise waters of Bacuit Bay, our haven offers an unmatched retreat for discerning travelers seeking serenity, sunset views, and world-class hospitality.

Exquisite Suites

Immaculately designed accommodations featuring private plunge pools, blue velvet accents, and panoramic views of Bacuit Bay.

Cliffside Fine Dining

Savor French-Mediterranean culinary artistry infused with fresh Palawan seafood at L'Horizon Restaurant & Sunset Bar.

Unmatched Serenity

Experience personalized butler service, private lagoon access, and holistic oceanfront spa treatments in complete privacy.

A Harmony of Two Worlds

The Heritage

Founded with a vision to marry European coastal elegance with authentic Palawan warmth, Casa Maison Bleue stands as an architectural tribute to timeless luxury. Every detail—from terracotta tilework to our signature deep blue textiles—reflects a passion for curated living.

Whether sipping vintage champagne at our cliffside Sunset Bar or exploring hidden lagoons in private yachts, our guests experience a refined escape designed to restore mind and spirit.

The Culinary Vision

At L’Horizon Restaurant, our executive culinary team reimagines classic Mediterranean recipes using locally harvested island spices, organic produce, and daily catches straight from Bacuit Bay.

Preserving Paradise

Our Environmental Commitment

El Nido is one of the world's most delicate marine ecosystems. We are committed to protecting this natural treasure for generations to come.

Zero Single-Use Plastics

We utilize glass bottling plants and biodegradable materials across all resort amenities.

Coral Reef Protection

Partnering with local marine biologists to sponsor and protect Bacuit Bay’s coral sanctuaries.

Farm-to-Table Dining

Supporting Palawan farmers and ethical fisheries to reduce footprint and support local communities.
