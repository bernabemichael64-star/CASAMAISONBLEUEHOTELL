Restaurant & Coastal Gastronomy

Operating Hours:

• Lunch Session: 12:00 PM – 2:30 PM

Casa Maison Bleue Fine Dining delivers an elite culinary experience situated on a cliffside coastal terrace in El Nido. Designed for guests seeking ultimate sophistication and romantic ambiance, the restaurant pairs breathtaking views of Bacuit Bay with meticulous tablescapes, ambient evening light, and curated culinary service.

Signature Features & Atmosphere
  • Dine by the Edge: Open-air terrace seating perched right on the cliffside, offering sweeping, unobstructed island and sunset vistas.
  • Architectural Charm: Framed by authentic stone arches, warm wall sconces, and lush tropical bougainvillea accents.
  • Fine Linens & Gold Cutlery: Tables draped in crisp, white linen topped with gold-finish flatware and delicate crystal stemware.
  • Casa Maison Bleue Signature: An elegant entrance featuring hand-painted ceramic tile signage, a solid carved wood door, and a polished brass crest plaque.
  • Evening Ambiance: Soft, glowing candle lanterns positioned on stone parapets, creating a warm, romantic atmosphere as twilight falls.
  • In-House Sommelier Selection: A glass-fronted, temperature-controlled wine display built directly into the stone architecture, featuring premier local and international vintages.
Curated Tasting Menu
  • Amuse-Bouche — Davao Mango & Sea Urchin Tartlet A delicate, buttery pastry shell filled with rich, creamy sea urchin (uni) harvested from local waters, balanced by the sweet acidity of ripe Davao mangoes and finished with translucent pearls of refreshing calamansi caviar.
  • Poisson (Fish Course) — Pan-Seared Palawan Lapu-Lapu Sustainably caught local red grouper pan-seared to crisp perfection, resting on a velvet bed of coconut-latik foam and nutty native wild rice, delivering a modern coastal interpretation of traditional island warmth.
  • Viande (Meat Course) — Slow-Roasted Batangas Wagyu Ribeye Melt-in-your-mouth Batangas Wagyu beef slow-roasted over low heat, glazed with an aromatic reduction of rich local Kapeng Barako (coffee), and accented with fresh garden herbs for a bold, savory finish.
  • Dessert — Guimaras Mango & Passion Fruit Soufflé An airy, golden soufflé made from world-renowned sweet Guimaras mangoes and tart passion fruit, crowned with delicate edible gold leaf and accompanied by a fragrant, floral sampaguita-infused sorbet.
